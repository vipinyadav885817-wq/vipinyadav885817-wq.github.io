Privacy policy

Updated 23 November 2026

Reading without an account

You can read public lessons without signing in. Reading progress marked in the library is saved in local storage on the browser you use. It does not sync to another device unless you use an account feature.

Optional account features

If you create an account, authentication and related account features are handled through Supabase. Use a unique password and do not share it. Account-related data is used to provide sign-in and progress features.

Advertising and cookies

CyberShield Academy is preparing to show ads through Google AdSense; ads may not appear until Google approves the site. If ads are served, Google and participating vendors may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar identifiers to collect information for serving, measuring, and personalizing ads. This may include information about prior visits to this site or other sites, depending on settings and applicable laws. Learn how Google uses data on partner sites, how AdSense uses cookies, and manage ad personalization settings. Analytics are not active on this site.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the project repository. Issues are public, so do not include passwords, payment details, or other private information.
