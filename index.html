GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need prior experience? +

No. Paths begin with approachable fundamentals, then progress through examples and advanced topics. Choose a subject and learn at your own pace.

Do I need an account to read lessons? +

No. Every learning path and lesson is open to read without signing in. You can mark lessons complete on this browser; an account is optional for your separate progress dashboard.

How are accounts and progress protected? +

Accounts use Supabase Auth with email confirmation and password reset. Lesson progress is stored with your account, and database rules restrict it to you. Use a unique password.

Are the security lessons intended for real-world systems? +

Learning should stay ethical and authorized. Only practice on systems you own or have explicit permission to test, preferably in a dedicated training lab.
